About me

Since 2023, I have been a PhD student in mathematics at the University of Maryland, College Park. I am interested in model theory and applications of model theory to combinatorics.

Before this, I was an MMathPhil student at the University of Oxford. My Part C dissertation in mathematics (supervised by Ehud Hrushovski) was on Kim-independence and vector spaces with billinear forms.

In the summer of 2022, I worked with Pantelis Eleftheriou at the University of Leeds on topics related to VC-dimension. The outcome of this work can be found below.
